# Aeterna Architecture: The Seven-Layer Stack

Aeterna is architected as a vertically integrated stack of technologies that enforce constitutional governance.

## 🧱 The 7-Layer Stack

### Layer 1: Semantic Firewall
- **Role:** Policy Validation & Bias Detection.
- **Tech:** LLM multi-modal agents filtering legislative drafts against "Root-Logic" rules.
- **Mechanism:** Deny-by-default on any policy lacking evidence or containing identity-based discrimination.

### Layer 2: Economic Regulators
- **Role:** Fiduciary Guardrails.
- **Tech:** Smart contracts bound to the "Harm Function" ($H$).
- **Mechanism:** Automatic freezing of disbursement flows if Gini coefficient or hunger indices spike above tolerance.

### Layer 3: Recursive Success Engine
- **Role:** Pattern Replication.
- **Tech:** Genetic algorithms + Neural network pattern matching.
- **Mechanism:** Extracts "Success DNA" from high-performing nodes and pushes them as templates to underperforming ones.

### Layer 4: Self-Correction Loop
- **Role:** Entropy Resistance.
- **Tech:** State-reversion checkpoints on a Byzantine fault-tolerant ledger.
- **Mechanism:** If corruption or policy capture is detected, the zone reverts to the last known "Stable Equilibrium" state.

### Layer 5: Automated Jurisprudence
- **Role:** Enforcement.
- **Tech:** Automated case-pipeline.
- **Mechanism:** Anomalies detected in Layer 4 are automatically packaged into digital evidence bundles and served to the judiciary.

### Layer 6: Public Truth-Ledger
- **Role:** Radical Transparency.
- **Tech:** Distributed Immutable Ledger.
- **Mechanism:** Real-time dashboard of all state expenditures and metric fluctuations, accessible to any citizen-auditor.

### Layer 7: Decentralized Network
- **Role:** Physical Residency.
- **Tech:** Federated node clusters.
- **Mechanism:** Low-bandwidth mesh-net synchronization to ensure the protocol works even in disconnected or hostile environments.

## 🏗 System Philosophy
1. **The Village is the Root:** Data and decisions flow from the community node up, not the center down.
2. **Immutable Logic:** Policies are code. Once passed through the Firewall, they are enforced without human discretion.
3. **Entropy Awareness:** Systems naturally decay. Layer 4 must pro-actively "cleanse" the state regularly.
