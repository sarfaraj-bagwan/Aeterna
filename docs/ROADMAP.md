# Aeterna Implementation Roadmap & Strategy

## 1. Five-Year Rollout Timeline

| Phase | Timeline | Focus | Key Milestones |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1: Foundation** | Month 1-12 | Infrastructure & Pilots | Establish 100 Village Nodes; Deploy Semantic Firewall core. |
| **Phase 2: Integration** | Month 13-24 | Success DNA Scaling | Activate Layer 3 (Cloning); Achieve 1,000 node saturation. |

## 2. Political Economy: Stakeholder Mapping

### A. The Reform Coalition (Pro-Aeterna)
- **Citizens:** Direct empowerment and improved affordability ($MEH$ metrics).
- **Civil Society:** Transparency ($Layer 6$) as a tool for advocacy.
- **Judiciary:** Reduced case backlog through automated filtering.
- **Village Councils:** Constitutional devolution of budgetary power.

### B. The Resistance (Anti-Aeterna)
- **Central Bureaucracy:** Loss of discretionary "gatekeeper" power.
- **Entrenched Elites:** Transparency limits rent-seeking and corruption.
- **Sectarian Groups:** Semantic Firewall ($Layer 1$) blocks divisive rhetoric.

## 3. Leverage Points for Transition
1.  **Grant Conditionality:** Central grants provided only to districts that adopt the Truth-Ledger.
2.  **Early Win Showcase:** Highlighting a village that reversed unemployment using "Success DNA".
3.  **Digital Literacy Jathas:** Community-led training to make the system citizen-autitable.

## 4. Legislative Roadmap
- **2027:** *Digital Governance Accountability Act* - Mandates blockchain budget tracking.
- **2028:** *Village Economic Sovereignty Act* - Constitutional shift in resource control.
